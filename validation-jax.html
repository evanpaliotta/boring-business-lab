The point of this lab is to help boring businesses in Northeast Florida. How, exactly, is still open — so this pack does not pitch a business. It maps which local trades are actually short of something, and what each one is short of. Four are thin. Six are served. One paid pull then corrected the free scan on the two things that decide everything: review depth and search demand.

What this lab is for now

read this before the tables

Goal: help boring, capital-light service businesses in Northeast Florida — the ones with a truck, a phone and no ops layer. Mechanism: undecided. Three candidates survive the data, and they are not the same business:

  1. After-hours intake & dispatch. One local number that answers when the owner is elbow-deep, qualifies the job, quotes a range, routes to whoever can actually serve today. Fits the fringe well-pump and marine trades. Revenue: per dispatched job.
  2. Appointment setting for consultative trades. Book the measure or quote, not the callback. Fits hurricane shutters. Revenue: per booked appointment.
  3. Availability brokering. Know who is free this week and place the work — the Laredo lesson without the Laredo market. Fits boat detailing's one-person calendars and post-storm marine work. Revenue: per placement.

What picks between them: one week of calls, not more research. Any mechanism needs the same first evidence — an operator saying, in their own words, that they lost a bookable job and what it cost. The kill criteria at the bottom of this page are written to produce that answer or end the thread.

Two corrections to the free scan

Boat detailing is the genuinely thin bench at 17. Two: every Jacksonville-specific search term in all four trades sits at 10–90 searches a month. A lead-volume product would have almost no leads to sell. That rules out the lead-site model for the whole metro and leaves relationship work.

Kill criteria

one week of calls decides this

Well pump, fringe intake. Kill if the edge operators say failures already reach a live person, or that their work comes entirely from builder and plumber relationships. Keep if two or more describe the same miss — call came in, nobody answered, customer rang the next name — and one names what a booked job is worth.

Boat detailing. Kill if the mobile operators say the season is already full and they want no more. Keep if they lose work to whoever answers first, or want the off-season filled and will pay per booked detail.

Hurricane shutters. Kill if the specialist says the measure calendar is booked through the season anyway. Keep if quote requests sit unanswered in the pre-storm rush — that is appointment setting, priced per booked measure.

Marine salvage. Kill unless an operator confirms missed mobilisations they cannot cover. Seasonal, insurer-adjacent, and you would be brokering availability you do not control — the weakest of the four.

What would change these verdicts

the honest open list

Four things this pack does not know

  • Whether the search volume is really that low. Serpstat has no geo parameter. A local-pack impression pull or Google Ads geo report would settle it.
  • Whether the fringe operators want the work. Two listings in Orange Park could mean thin supply or no demand. Only the phone distinguishes them.
  • What a booked job is worth in each trade. That number sets the ceiling for any per-job fee, and no listing data contains it.
  • Whether boat detailing's low review count means young businesses or bad operators. Review depth alone cannot tell those apart.

Method & limits

what this is and is not

Source: Google Maps local list view via browser (free), 169 queries across 10 services and 30 metros, 22–23 Oct 2026; plus a paid pull of 53 listings with review counts (Google Places, 7 calls) and 20 keywords (Serpstat). Total spend $0.0135.

Regions. "Core" is Duval, Jacksonville Beach, St Johns, Clay and Nassau. "Fringe" is the drivable NE Florida ring — Callahan, Yulee, Macclenny, Middleburg, Green Cove Springs, St Augustine, Amelia. Comparison metros outside the region (Gainesville, Ocala, Tallahassee, Brunswick, Waycross, Douglas, Valdosta, the Carolinas) were scanned only to test whether a finding was local or generic, and are not prospects.

Review counts are partial. Only the 53 listings in the paid pull carry them. The 508-listing grid does not, so the ten-trade table says "not pulled" rather than implying zero.

Duplicates and drift. The same operator appears across metros and sometimes under two numbers; gap tables dedupe by name, market counts do not. Repeated local-pack calls returned different counts and medians — treat every number as a snapshot that orients a call.

This is not a market study. No revenue, no lead volume, no capacity, no pricing. All of that requires the phone.
