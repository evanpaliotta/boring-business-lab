Kill criteria

decide before dialling — one per gap

Well pump repair. Kill if the operators say the pump failure calls already reach a live person after hours, or if the ones with no website tell you they get all their work from a builder/plumber relationship and want no more. Keep if two or more describe the same failure (call came in, nobody answered, customer called the next name) and one names what a booked job is worth.

Boat detailing. Kill if the mobile operators say their calendar is full in season and empty off-season with no interest in filling it. Keep if they describe losing jobs to whoever answers first, or want the off-season filled and will pay per booked detail.

Hurricane shutters. Kill if the one specialist says the measure/quote calendar is booked through the season anyway. Keep if they say quote requests sit unanswered during the pre-storm rush — that is appointment setting, priced per booked measure.

Marine salvage. Kill unless an operator confirms the surge creates missed mobilisations they cannot cover. This is the weakest of the four: seasonal, insurer-adjacent, and you would be brokering availability you do not control.

Method & limits

what this is and is not

Source: Google Maps local list view via browser, 170 queries across 10 services and 30 metros, captured 22–23 Oct 2026. Names, phones, ratings and website presence as displayed. Zero data-provider spend.

Review counts were added on 23 Oct. The first pass used the free list view, which does not expose review depth; a paid Places pull (7 queries, $0.006) and a keyword pull (20 terms, $0.0075) now back the review and demand tables. Every raw response is saved as data/raw_places_*.json and data/kw_raw.txt so nothing has to be re-bought.

Regions matter. "Core" is Duval, Jacksonville Beach, St Johns, Clay and Nassau — your backyard. "Fringe" is the drivable NE Florida ring. "Out" cells (Gainesville, Ocala, Tallahassee, Brunswick, Waynesville/Waycross, the Carolinas) exist as comparison only and are not prospects.

Duplicates. The same operator appears across metros and occasionally under two phone numbers. Gap tables are deduped by name; market counts are not.

Samples drift. Repeated local-pack calls for the same query returned different counts and medians. Treat every number as a snapshot that orients a phone call, not a measurement.

Raw data: data/nefl-supply-scan.csv (one row per operator listing), data/nefl-supply-markets.csv (market aggregates), data/nefl-supply-scan.json (both + verdicts), rebuildable with data/build_scan.py.
